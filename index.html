🤖 How FloodGuard AI Works

From Earth observation to community awareness

01 — NASA Earth Observation

Earth observation imagery can help visualize environmental conditions, water and land changes.

02 — Environmental Data

Rainfall, water and weather information can be combined to identify areas requiring further attention.

03 — AI Risk Model

A future machine-learning model could combine multiple environmental variables to calculate a risk score.

04 — Community Awareness

The system could present complex environmental information in a simple format for communities.
